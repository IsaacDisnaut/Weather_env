## The Khanom Buang Merit-Making

In the set: painting 2 of 12
Ceremony of: First month
Scene: Amarin Winitchai Throne Hall
Located on: the wall of the main vihara, Wat Rajpradit

![The Khanom Buang Merit-Making](mural.jpg)

The date was never fixed in advance: it waited for the season when shrimp were
rich enough to fill the khanom buang. Eighty monks were received in the Amarin
Winitchai Throne Hall, while nobles and palace staff set up griddles under an
awning beside the hall.

In the mural a senior monk carrying a ceremonial fan walks with a group of
temple boys toward the Thewet Raksa gate, where officials wait to receive them.
To the side, the women of the inner court work the griddles in full swing.

## The sensor at this painting

Device: dev-B
Placement: mounted beside the painting (edit this file to match the real spot)

It measures temperature, humidity and vibration at the painted wall.
Swinging humidity and sustained vibration are the two things that crack and
lift the paint layer of a mural.
