## The Year-End Rite

In the set: painting 5 of 12
Ceremony of: Fourth into fifth month
Scene: Suan Dusit Maha Prasat
Located on: the wall of the main vihara, Wat Rajpradit

![The Year-End Rite](mural.jpg)

An annual rite for the good fortune of the capital. Rounded wooden staves called
krabong phet were handed out as protection against harm, and the atana cannon
was fired so that the noise would drive off malevolent spirits.

The mural shows the front of Suan Dusit Maha Prasat, monks climbing the hall to
chant the Atanatiya Paritta, and a Thai-style building in the western corner
thought to be the Ho Thamsangwet. Outside the palace wall, gunners fire the
atana cannon with flame bursting from the muzzle; inside it, palace women give
alms, and further off children fly chula and pakpao kites against each other.
