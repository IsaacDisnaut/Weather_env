## The Royal Kathin by Land

In the set: painting 12 of 12
Ceremony of: Eighth waning day, eleventh month
Scene: The land procession
Located on: the wall of the main vihara, Wat Rajpradit

![The Royal Kathin by Land](mural.jpg)

The royal Kathin began on the eighth waning day of the eleventh month by long
tradition — a major ceremony in which the king processed both by water and, as
here, by land.

The mural shows the Kathin robes carried on elephants and horses, much like the
procession for the blessing of the royal animals, with crowds pressing in on
both sides. Nearby, tables are laid for foreign guests of the court, and
officials stand with the offerings, paper flags planted among them to mark
them as the king's own Kathin goods.
