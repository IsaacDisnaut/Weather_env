## Floating the Royal Lanterns

In the set: painting 1 of 12
Ceremony of: Twelfth month
Scene: Tha Ratchaworadit, on the Chao Phraya
Located on: the wall of the main vihara, Wat Rajpradit

![Floating the Royal Lanterns](mural.jpg)

Held in the twelfth lunar month, a festival kept by court and commoners alike.
The mural shows the lanterns floated at night against Tha Ratchaworadit, west
of the Grand Palace, with four royal pavilions in view - Thippayasathan
Thepsathit, Chalangkhaphiman, Anong Nai Saranrom and Ratchakit Winitchai -
flanked by Fort In Amnuai Son on the right and Fort Phrom Sin on the left.
Only Ratchakit Winitchai still stands today.

On the right, two kanya-canopied barges are moored side by side as a royal
seat. Beside them is the krathong luang, a small gilded animal-shaped boat for
the royal lanterns, and the krathong yai offered by nobles and officials.
Below are boats carrying fireworks, torch floats and crowds of commoners.

## The sensor at this painting

Device: dev-A
Placement: mounted beside the painting (edit this file to match the real spot)

It measures temperature, humidity and vibration at the painted wall.
Swinging humidity and sustained vibration are the two things that crack and
lift the paint layer of a mural.
