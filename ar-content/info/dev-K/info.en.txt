## The Sart Rite and the Stirring of Khao Thip

In the set: painting 11 of 12
Ceremony of: Tenth month
Scene: The open halls on the eastern side
Located on: the wall of the main vihara, Wat Rajpradit

![The Sart Rite and the Stirring of Khao Thip](mural.jpg)

The rite recalls Visakha offering khao thip to the Buddha, and Annakondanya
offering yaku to a Buddha of the past and reaching arahantship before all
others — held to be auspicious and to ward off illness from nobles and
officials.

The mixture stirred was large: rice, grains, milk, cane juice, butter, fresh
and candied fruit. Only unmarried young women of the inner court, from royal
daughters down to mom chao, were allowed to stir it, dressed entirely in white.

On the eastern side of the mural stand two open halls. In the left one the
stirring takes place, the hall full of court women, with four fire pits in
front enclosed by a ceremonial fence and tiered flags; the women crouch in
their skirts working the paddles. On the right a group of senior monks carrying
ceremonial fans and shoulder bags files through the gate to chant.
