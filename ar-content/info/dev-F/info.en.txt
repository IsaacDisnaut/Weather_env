## Songkran and the Blessing of Elephants and Horses

In the set: painting 6 of 12
Ceremony of: Songkran
Scene: Chai Chumphon pavilion, Sanam Luang
Located on: the wall of the main vihara, Wat Rajpradit

![Songkran and the Blessing of Elephants and Horses](mural.jpg)

The Kachendrasawasanan rite, begun under Rama IV, blessed the war elephants and
horses that carried the kingdom into battle. A large ceremonial ground was laid
out at Sanam Luang, where the royal elephants, royal horses and other notable
animals were paraded past royal teachers, court scholars and Brahmins who
sprinkled holy water over the whole procession.

The scene is set before the Chai Chumphon pavilion, a small hall on the eastern
wall in front of Wat Phra Kaew built by Rama IV and flanked by two small forts,
with men and women seated outside the wall to watch.

Order of the procession: howdah elephant - the white monkey lord on a canopied
litter - the royal horse - the oxen - elephants in full regalia.
