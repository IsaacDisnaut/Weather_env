## The Start of Buddhist Lent

In the set: painting 9 of 12
Ceremony of: Eighth month
Scene: The alms platform at Wat Phra Kaew
Located on: the wall of the main vihara, Wat Rajpradit

![The Start of Buddhist Lent](mural.jpg)

For three months of the rains, while the crops come up, the monks stay within
their monasteries. Lay people take the chance to bring offerings to the temple,
and at court it was the custom for princes to be ordained at the ordination
hall of Wat Phra Kaew.

In the mural the prince being ordained wears a ceremonial crown and a gold
brocade robe as he steps onto the platform to scatter alms, an attendant
holding the royal umbrella behind him. Pages and servants scramble merrily for
the coins, with young women slipping in to compete with the men.
