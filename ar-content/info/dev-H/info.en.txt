## The Salakaphat Offering

In the set: painting 8 of 12
Ceremony of: Seventh month
Scene: The procession from the Grand Palace to Wat Rajpradit
Located on: the wall of the main vihara, Wat Rajpradit

![The Salakaphat Offering](mural.jpg)

Rama IV added this rite in the seventh month, a gap otherwise empty of royal
ceremonies. In the salakaphat the Buddha allowed monks to draw lots for the
food and goods that lay people brought to offer.

The procession was deliberately festive: court theatre troupes or young women
in fine dress, and sometimes carts of savoury and sweet dishes and seasonal
fruit.

The mural follows the procession from the rear of the Grand Palace to Wat
Rajpradit, led by lute and piphat ensembles. Children with topknots and gold
ornaments head the line, followed by young women in pairs with flowers at their
ears, shouldering the offerings — and watched closely by the young men who came
along for exactly that reason.
