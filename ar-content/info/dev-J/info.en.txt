## The Rain-Making Rite

In the set: painting 10 of 12
Ceremony of: Ninth month
Scene: Sanam Luang
Located on: the wall of the main vihara, Wat Rajpradit

![The Rain-Making Rite](mural.jpg)

An offering for rain, performed only when the rains failed to arrive on time —
in a normal year it was left undone, on the belief that it would only make the
weather worse. Like the First Ploughing, it was held at Sanam Luang.

The mural shows two open pavilions side by side: one enshrining the Buddha
image for the rite, the other where the rite itself was performed, with a wind
tower to draw the breeze down onto them. To the north stand a small pavilion
for watching the fields and a theatre for the propitiatory performances, ringed
by a low wall. Outside it, two-storey granaries holding the royal rice stand in
a row around the pavilions.

## The sensor at this painting

Placement: mounted beside the painting (edit this file to match the real spot)

Swinging humidity and sustained vibration are the two things that crack and
lift the paint layer of a mural.
